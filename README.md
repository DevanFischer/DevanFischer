# Hi, [I'm Devan! ](https://www.devanfischer.com)<img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/Q7LHmoFwVP6Yc1swZs/giphy.gif" width=30>

### Welcome to my collection of digital adventures..

I am a US Army Veteran 🎖️ and Applied AI Engineer with a background in software engineering and automation. I combine technical depth with a drive to make AI accessible and practical.

I currently work at [LearnAIR](https://www.learnair.com) as an AI Educator and Developer, building interactive tools and AI education initiatives that help people understand and apply AI in real-world contexts. My focus spans AI systems, agent design, and end-to-end implementation — from concept to production.
